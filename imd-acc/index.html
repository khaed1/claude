imd/acc
Design draft · Oct 2026
Trading cashback for the IMD ecosystem

Every trade stacks staked IMD for you.

Participating projects send 0.5% of every trade back to the trader as sIMD, deposited straight into IMD's staking vault in their name. No claim, no extra fee, and it keeps earning.

How it works

  1. You trade in a participating pool, say PLEA/IMD, through the project's site or any router that reaches the pool.
  2. The hook takes 0.5% on the IMD side of your trade. On IMD-paired pools there's nothing to swap, so it costs little gas and has no slippage.
  3. It deposits that IMD into the sIMD vault for you with deposit(amount, you). The vault mints the shares straight to your wallet.
  4. Your sIMD balance goes up. Open the staking page or your wallet and it's there, growing with the vault's yield. Unstake whenever you like.

Why it holds up

Nobody holds your cashback

imd/acc never takes custody. Shares are minted directly to the trader's address.

The vault is renounced

sIMD's owner() reads 0x0 and it isn't paused, checked on-chain on 9 Oct 2026. Nobody can pause it or sweep it.

No fee on top

Projects fund the 0.5% from fees they already charge. Traders pay nothing extra.

For projects

Any IMD-paired project can join with one call from its hook. Projects without a hook can use the drop-in imd/acc hook.

// in your hook's afterSwap, with the trader taken from hookData
uint256 cashback = imdSideAmount * 50 / 10_000;   // 0.5%
stacker.credit(trader, cashback);                  // → sIMD.deposit(cashback, trader)
  • Pass the real trader in hookData and fall back to sender, never tx.origin.
  • On Ethereum, the hook can save cashback per trader and deposit in batches to save gas.
  • Every credit emits Stacked(project, trader, imd, shares) for leaderboards.
