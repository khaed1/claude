## Findings

### 1. Low: A stranger's $PONDPAD sent to MarketController before migrate is folded into the new market position (cap raised, backstop IMD consumed) instead of being burned

`launchpad/contracts/src/MarketController.sol:349`

```
        uint128 liquidity = fullRangeLiquidity(sqrtPriceX96, imdBal, tokenBal, nh.tickSpacing());
```

`migrate` sizes the new full-range position from the controller's whole IMD and $PONDPAD balances after `old.closeMarket` (`imdBal`, `tokenBal`, lines 346-350) and burns only what does not fit. The IMD balance includes the old market's retained and backstop IMD, so the token side is binding and every $PONDPAD on the controller goes into the position with a matching slice of that IMD. Anyone can transfer $PONDPAD to the controller (nothing sweeps it between launch and migrate; `approveMigration` announces a migration 7 days ahead), so an unprivileged sender enlarges the position, lifts `inventoryCap` to the larger `tokensDeposited` (above `oldCap`, which `inheritGuards` then keeps) and converts part of the buy wall below the price into full-range liquidity at the current price. This contradicts `fundInventory`'s NatSpec (lines 260-261: tokens already on the controller are burned 'as in launch and migrate'), `migrate`'s own NatSpec ('the controller keeps nothing') and ARCHITECTURE 5.4.1 / THREAT-MODEL invariant 11, where only the 48 h owner raises the cap (through `fundInventory`). Nothing leaves the protocol and the sender pays the donation, so this is griefing of the burn programme (trims delayed by donation / 500k per day of decay) within a listed power's effect: Low. Fix: in `migrate`, before `old.closeMarket`, send whatever the controller already holds to the fee splitter (IMD) and to the burner ($PONDPAD, then `burn()`), as `launch` and `fundInventory` do, so the position and the backstop seed are sized only from what the close returned. Merged: reported by audit_economics only; verified by running its proof and an independent probe.

**Reproduction**

MarketBase setup (test/Market.t.sol): PoolManager, $PONDPAD mined above IMD, sale graduated at 8,460 IMD / 300M $PONDPAD, cap floor 150M, decay 500k/day. ### 2. Info: Seeded or owner-returned backstop IMD still pays the keeper fill tip once the band it was deployed into fills; invariant 11 and the untippedQuote NatSpec read as if it never earns a tip

`launchpad/contracts/src/PadMarketHook.sol:734`

```
        uint256 tip = _keeperRewardDue(idleReady ? tippable : 0, converted);
```

The R2-A2-1 fix (`untippedQuote`) removes the tip only on the idle-deployment leg: `tippable` excludes IMD that came back from an owner `closeBackstop` or a migration `seedRetainedQuote`. The fill leg is unchanged: `_keeperRewardDue` takes `converted` (IMD principal of the live band that a dump turned into tokens) as work value, and once the untipped IMD has been deployed as the band `untippedQuote` is clamped to 0 (line 736), so when that band fills the next `rebalance()` pays min(`keeperReward`, `currentFee()` x converted) out of retained IMD that originated from the seed or the owner close. THREAT-MODEL invariant 11 ('IMD returned by an owner closeBackstop or seeded by a migration earns no keeper tip'), ARCHITECTURE 5.4.1 (`closeBackstop` row) and the `untippedQuote` NatSpec state no such exception. The actual bound is POOL4's: the tip is at most the pool fee on the converted IMD, and the dump that converted it paid at least that much fee to the protocol (below: ~1.46M $PONDPAD, ~41 IMD at the pool price, in fees for a 1 IMD tip), so no value can be extracted and the R2-A2-1 loop (closeBackstop + rebalance with no trade) stays closed. Documentation only: say in invariant 11 / the NatSpec that the untipped IMD earns no idle-deployment tip but a later fill of the band it funds pays the ordinary fee-bounded fill tip; or, if the stricter reading is wanted, carry an untipped share into `backstopQuotePrincipal` and subtract it from `converted` in `_keeperRewardDue` (an owner design choice, since it also stops tipping a legitimate fill). Merged: reported by audit_flow; verified with a scratch probe (both the migration-seed and the owner-closeBackstop path).
