# Toast

Transaction results, bottom right (desktop) or above the tab bar (phone), 6 seconds, dismissible. `is-ok` after a confirmed transaction with a link to the explorer or the new coin; `is-error` with what went wrong and what to do. Pending transactions show in the button, not a toast. Copy: "It's alive. Your tadpole is swimming.", "That didn't go through. Nothing was lost except a bit of gas."
