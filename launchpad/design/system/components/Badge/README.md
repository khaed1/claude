# Badge

Small facts about a coin. `pp-badge-x` (lotus): the creator linked the coin's X account through `SocialRegistry` (tooltip: "This means the creator controls this X account. It doesn't mean the coin is safe. Nothing does."). `pp-badge-chorus` (lotus): the site was built by the swarm (links to the job). `pp-badge-tax`: the coin tax and where it goes ("Tax 0.5% → holders"); show it whenever tax > 0. `pp-badge-warn` (coral): a takeover notice or a flagged duplicate handle. Plain `pp-badge` for neutral facts ("Paid in ETH"). At most two on a coin card (X verified, Site by the Chorus); the coin page shows all.
