# Testnet

PondPad runs on **Robinhood Chain Testnet** (chain 46630) while it's being audited. Everything works as on mainnet, with test tokens that have no value:

- **Test IMD and test USDG** come from the [faucet](/faucet): 500 tIMD and 5,000 tUSDG per wallet per hour.
- **Testnet ETH** for gas: get Sepolia ETH from a faucet and bridge it to Robinhood Chain Testnet.
- The timelocks are 10 and 30 minutes instead of 48 hours and 7 days, so changes can be tested in one sitting.

Found something odd? That's what the testnet is for. Tell us on X.
