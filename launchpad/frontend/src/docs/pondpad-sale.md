# The $PONDPAD sale

$PONDPAD sells on a bonding curve, the same way every coin here starts. No presale, no VCs, no whitelist.

- **600,000,000 $PONDPAD** (60% of the supply) are sold on the curve. The price rises as it fills.
- The curve is full when it has raised its target, **about 8,460 IMD**. That buy opens the market in the same transaction: the IMD the curve raised and **300,000,000 $PONDPAD** go into the pool at the curve's last price.
- Pay with **IMD, ETH or USDG**; ETH and USDG are swapped to IMD in the same transaction. You can sell back to the curve anytime before the Leap.
- Every trade pays a **1% fee** to the protocol fee split (40% stakers, 25% IMD workers, 20% growth, 15% treasury).
- **Early-bird tax:** buys in the first 30 minutes pay a tax that starts at 80% and falls evenly to zero. It goes to growth. It exists so bots don't get the cheapest tokens; waiting is cheaper.
- **Per-wallet limit:** each wallet can buy up to **15,000,000 $PONDPAD** over the whole sale. Selling doesn't free up room.

Where the rest goes: 5% airdrop to the IMD community, 3% kept for adding liquidity later (held by the 48-hour timelock), 2% team (nothing for the first month after the Leap, fully released by month 6).
