# Risks

- **Coins can go to zero.** Anyone can spawn a coin, including people who want to mislead you. Badges such as "X verified" mean the creator linked that account, not that the coin is safe.
- **Prices move fast.** Slippage and price impact can be large on thin curves and pools, and bots may trade before you.
- **Smart contracts can have bugs.** PondPad's contracts are audited by the swarm, round after round, and tested on a fork of the live chain and on the testnet. That reduces risk; it does not remove it.
- **Other systems can fail.** Robinhood Chain, Uniswap v4, IMD, USDG, wallets, RPCs and IPFS gateways are outside PondPad's control.
- **Rules can change only slowly and in public.** Settings changes wait 48 hours or 7 days in public timelocks, and they apply to future launches only. A coin's fees, curve and target never change after launch.
- **Transactions are final.** Nobody, including us, can reverse one.
