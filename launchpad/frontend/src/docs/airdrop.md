# The airdrop

**5% of $PONDPAD (50,000,000)** goes to the IMD community: 70% to active IMD workers (seats with real recent work), 30% to IMD holders with at least 7,000 IMD. The snapshot is taken in secret and announced only after it's taken. The list, each wallet's amount and its proof are published, so anyone can check them against the Merkle root fixed in the contract.

## Waking the pond

The airdrop sleeps until **100 wallets from the list** wake it, and that can only start after the $PONDPAD market opens. To wake it:

1. Connect the wallet that's on the list. The $PONDPAD page shows **your code**: 12 characters derived from your wallet and the airdrop contract, so anyone can check a post against the wallet that sent it.
2. Post the line the page gives you on X.
3. Paste the link to your post. Our tweet checker reads it and signs a voucher; your wallet sends it. One X account and one post per wallet.

When the 100th wallet wakes it, **everyone on the list** can claim, not just the 100.

## Claiming

Your share unlocks evenly over **30 days** from the moment the airdrop woke. Claim as often as you like. Claims stay open for **180 days**; whatever is left then goes to the Pond (stakers).

## A claim wallet, without gas

Want to keep the listed wallet off the trading side, or it has no ETH on Robinhood Chain? On the $PONDPAD page, name a claim wallet and **sign once** (no transaction). You get a link; open it with the claim wallet connected and it sets itself and claims in one transaction. Later claims from either wallet pay the claim wallet. The signature works once, for that wallet only, and expires after 7 days.
