# Your first trade

1. **Connect a wallet** (top right) and switch to Robinhood Chain. Accept the Terms and Privacy Policy once per browser session.
2. **Pick a coin** on Explore. Tadpoles show how far they are from the Leap; frogs have leapt.
3. In the **trade box**, choose Buy or Sell and what you pay with or receive: IMD, ETH or USDG.
4. Enter an amount or tap a preset. The box shows **what you get at least**, the **route** (for example ETH → IMD → $COIN), the **price impact** and the **fee**, before you sign.
5. Press **Buy** (or Sell). Paying with IMD or USDG first asks your wallet to approve that exact amount; ETH needs no approval.

**Slippage** is how far the price can move before your trade gives up. 1% is the default. Raise it a little if a trade fails because the price moved.

**Early-bird tax.** In the first seconds after a coin launches, buys pay an extra tax that falls to zero (70% → 0 over 80 seconds on mainnet; 50% → 0 over 20 seconds on the testnet) and each wallet can buy at most 2% of the supply while the coin is an Egg (the first 80 seconds on mainnet, 60 on the testnet). It's there to keep bots honest. Selling is never restricted.
