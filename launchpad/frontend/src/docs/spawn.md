# Spawn a coin

Spawning takes one transaction and costs a **0.35 IMD** launch fee on mainnet (1 IMD on the testnet) plus gas. You can pay with IMD, ETH or USDG.

- **Name and ticker:** 1–32 and 1–12 characters. They can't be changed later.
- **Image, description and links:** shown on the coin page. Links must be https or ipfs.
- **Coin tax (optional):** 0–3% on every trade, on top of the 1.5% base fee, split as you choose between **you**, **holders** (paid as IMD dividends) and the coin's **swarm budget** (pays the swarm for work on your coin). Fixed forever at launch.
- **Dev buy (optional):** buy your own coin in the same transaction, before anyone else, without the early-bird tax.

Every coin has a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, no owner and no mint. 800M are sold on the curve; 200M go into the pool at the Leap.

**Creator fees.** You earn 0.5% of every trade (plus your part of the coin tax, if any). They collect in the creator vault; claim them from your **Profile → Created**, or on the coin page.
