# Privacy Policy

*Version 2026-10-05. Draft: to be reviewed by counsel before mainnet.*

This policy explains what information the PondPad website (the "Interface"), operated by [PondPad operator legal name] ("we"), handles, and why. We keep it short because we collect very little: there are no accounts, no passwords, no emails and no tracking cookies.

## 1. What we handle

| Information | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Your wallet address** and its public onchain activity | Your wallet, when you connect, and the public blockchain | To show your balances, coins and claims, and to prepare transactions for you to sign. It is public data on Robinhood Chain |
| **Your acceptance of these terms** | A session cookie set when you accept | To not ask you again during the same browser session. It holds the wallet address and the version you accepted, and is deleted when the session ends or you clear cookies |
| **Preferences** (theme, slippage, pay-with token) | Your browser's local storage | To remember your choices on this device. Never sent to us |
| **Technical request data** (IP address, user agent, time) | Our hosting provider, RPC providers and IPFS gateways, when your browser loads the Interface or reads the chain | To deliver the Interface and keep it secure. We do not combine it with wallet addresses or use it for profiling |
| **X (Twitter) handle and post links**, if you choose to link an X account or initiate the airdrop | You, through the X link service or the airdrop form | To sign the onchain voucher that links the handle, or to verify the airdrop post. Linked handles are published onchain by design |
| **Coin details you publish** (name, ticker, image, description, links) | You, when you launch a coin | To launch the coin. This content is public and stored onchain or on IPFS, where we cannot delete it |

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent any information.

## 2. The blockchain is public and permanent

Everything you do through the Protocol (launches, trades, stakes, claims, links) is recorded on Robinhood Chain, which is public and cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including us. Your wallet address and its history can be seen by anyone, and may be linked to your identity by others.

## 3. Who else processes data

- **Hosting and content delivery** for the Interface ([hosting provider]).
- **RPC providers** (Robinhood Chain's public RPC or others) that answer your browser's chain queries.
- **IPFS gateways** that serve coin images and metadata.
- **X (Twitter)**, if you link an account.
- **Your wallet provider**, under its own policy.

Each processes data under its own terms. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it

We do not store wallet activity ourselves; the chain does. The acceptance cookie lasts for your browser session. Server logs of our hosting provider are kept for at most [30] days for security. Records of X links and airdrop verifications are kept as long as they are needed for the airdrop and the links they support.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold almost nothing and onchain data cannot be changed, most requests can be answered by clearing your browser's cookies and storage. For anything else, contact us at [contact email].

Legal bases (where the GDPR applies): providing the Interface you asked for (contract), keeping it secure (legitimate interest) and complying with law (legal obligation).

## 6. Children

The Interface is not for anyone under 18.

## 7. Changes

We will update this policy when what we handle changes. The version date is at the top, and the Interface asks you to accept a new version before you continue.
