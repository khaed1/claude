## 1. What PondPad is

PondPad is a set of open, non-custodial smart contracts on Robinhood Chain (the "Protocol") that let anyone launch tokens paired with IMD, trade them on a bonding curve and then in a Uniswap v4 pool, and stake $PONDPAD. The Interface is one way to read the Protocol and to prepare transactions for your own wallet to sign. Other interfaces, bots and apps can use the Protocol without us.

- **We never hold your funds or keys.** Every transaction is signed by your wallet and executed by the smart contracts. We cannot move, freeze, reverse or recover your assets.
- **Transactions are final.** Once confirmed onchain, a transaction cannot be undone, by us or anyone else.
- **The Protocol runs without us.** Its rules are in its code. The limited administrative powers that exist (for example changing settings for future launches) are held by public timelocks with fixed delays and are described in the Docs.

## 4. Fees

Using the Protocol costs fees set in its smart contracts, shown in the Interface before you sign:

- every trade pays a **1.5% base fee** (1% to the protocol, 0.5% to the coin's creator) plus any **coin tax** (0–3%) chosen by the creator at launch;
- a launch fee (currently 1 IMD), an optional website add-on, and, for buys right after a launch or during the $PONDPAD sale, a temporary early-bird tax;
- swaps between IMD, ETH and USDG pay the fees of the pools they go through, and every transaction pays network gas.

Protocol fees are distributed by the smart contracts as described in the Docs. Fees are not refundable.
