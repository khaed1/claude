DESIGN (Impeccable, github.com/pbakaus/impeccable, Apache-2.0; its rules are written here):
- Identity: the logo is the word PLEA as a typographic wordmark in the display face (tight tracking, no icon, no badge). Mood: a private court: ink, parchment and a wax-seal red, calm and expensive.
- Type: a distinctive self-hosted display face and a refined body face (e.g. Instrument Serif + Geist; never Inter, Arial or system fonts); mono only for addresses, hashes and numbers; tabular numerals; fluid type scale.
- Colour: a committed palette in tokens; tinted neutrals (never pure black or grey); state colours only where they carry meaning; real light and dark themes.
- Layout: 12-column grid, max width ~1200px, generous whitespace, one spacing scale, body measure 65-75ch.
- Icons: only where they carry meaning, from one set (Phosphor, one weight); no generated SVGs, emoji or unicode icons.
- One signature: the verdict stamp, set like a wax seal, with one authored motion (exponential ease-out) when a verdict appears; Laureates reuse the seal in gold. No other effects.
- Every control: hover, focus-visible, disabled, loading, error and empty states; themed selection, caret, scrollbars and focus rings; touch targets ≥44px; contrast ≥4.5:1.
- Refuse: nested cards, big-number hero templates, eyebrow labels, section numbers, gradient text, purple-blue gradients, glass or blur, side borders >1px, hard offset shadows, glows, bounce easing, modals where a page works.
