What it is: a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4. Anyone can buy. Selling needs a plea: an oracle panel (IMD, 30 judges, quorum 17) scores the plea 0-45, and it passes when the score reaches a need of 69 minus an on-chain fact score (0-41). An approved plea is one sale, within 7 minutes, through the gate. 33 hours without a verdict kills the gate for good and PLEA then trades freely. Nine contracts in src/, 3,332 nSLOC.

What it does: The hook owns the PLEA/IMD pool. It seeds 820M PLEA single-sided at a fixed market cap (14,000 IMD) and takes fees on every swap: 2.0% in IMD (0.5% cashback staked for the trader through imd/acc's Stacker, 1% to the owner, 0.25% to the season prize pot, 0.25% to a buy wall) plus 0.25% of the PLEA burned. For 90 minutes after the seed a launch window adds a falling extra fee and caps buys at 5M PLEA. While the gate's Cabal lives only the gate may sell, a buy needs a recipient (hookData or the router's msgSender) and exact-output buys are refused. An inventory cap ratchets down toward the pool's PLEA over 15 days, and PLEA a sale pushes above it is trimmed out of the liquidity and burned. A permissionless rebalance rebuilds a one-sided IMD wall just below the price, never above a guard price that rises at most 700 ticks a day. When the PoolManager cannot pay at once, value is kept as ERC-6909 claims (retainClaims, pleaClaims) and redeemed later.

Please look hardest at: Delta accounting in beforeSwap/afterSwap for exact-input and exact-output swaps in both directions, including partial fills at a price limit; the fee split at 1% to the owner; the claims paths and their redemption; the cap ratchet and the trim; the wall's placement, guard and settlement; reentrancy through the PoolManager unlock and the Stacker call; rounding that leaks value or bricks swaps; griefing rebalance or the trim; any way to sell around the gate while the Cabal lives.
