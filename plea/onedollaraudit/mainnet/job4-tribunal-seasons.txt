What it is: a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4. Anyone can buy. Selling needs a plea: an oracle panel (IMD, 30 judges, quorum 17) scores the plea 0-45, and it passes when the score reaches a need of 69 minus an on-chain fact score (0-41). An approved plea is one sale, within 7 minutes, through the gate. 33 hours without a verdict kills the gate for good and PLEA then trades freely. Nine contracts in src/, 3,332 nSLOC.

What it does: Objection! (Tribunal): for 20 minutes after an approval anyone but the seller and the excluded wallets may object, staking 0.35 IMD and paying the 0.5 IMD oracle fee. A fresh panel re-scores the plea: sustained when newScore + 2 < need (stake back, the plea earns no points), otherwise overruled (the lowest score counts, the stake goes to the plea's season pot); no answer in 20 minutes voids it (stake back). At most two objections per plea, from different wallets; the sale itself is never touched. Seasons (15 days on mainnet): record(pleaId) stores an approved plea's score (x1.2 for holders of the identity.md seat NFT); each wallet's best three count; closeSeason pays the top 25 wallets with at least 60 points from that season's prize fees plus rollover, plus a 10M PLEA bonus in each of the first six seasons; the owner never plays; after the Cabal dies, flushToWall sends what is left to the hook's buy wall.

Please look hardest at: Stake custody and settlement; double or out-of-order rulings; fees credited to the wrong season, rollover and closed seasons; the fail-open external reads (the seat NFT, the Tribunal from Seasons); anything that lets someone take the pot, block a close, or earn points for a sustained or unrecorded plea; the lowest approved score is now 28 (69 minus at most 41).
