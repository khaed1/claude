Foundry, solc 0.8.26, via_ir, cancun; deps in lib/ (Uniswap v4-core, OpenZeppelin, solmate). This is the code we plan to launch on Ethereum mainnet; the mainnet build changes only settings (a 14,000 IMD opening market cap instead of 5,700, 15-day seasons instead of 5 hours, the oracle fee bought in IMD instead of minted from a test token).

What it is: a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4. Anyone can buy. Selling needs a plea: an oracle panel (IMD, 30 judges, quorum 17) scores the plea 0-45, and it passes when the score reaches a need of 71 minus an on-chain fact score (0-45). An approved plea is one sale, within 7 minutes, through the gate. 33 hours without a verdict kills the gate for good and PLEA then trades freely. Nine contracts in src/, 3,399 nSLOC.

What it does: Letters: a holder writes a letter (plain text). The Letterbox escrows 20% of their PLEA (within the gate's 2.5M cap), pays the 0.5 oracle fee (minted from a test token on Sepolia; on mainnet bought by selling a slice of the escrow), files the plea through the gate, and on approval delivers the sale automatically (a 95% floor taken after the hook's 2% fees). settle returns the escrow and the tip when no verdict can come. Letters can appeal (same share as the original, never below the gate's minimum). It also keeps names and builds the status and leaderboard text.

The token and the launch: PLEA is an ERC-20 (Ownable2Step) whose transfers are restricted while the Cabal lives: only the gate's pull, the court escrow and exempt senders move tokens. IMD's factory deploys PLEA, CabalGate, Seasons, PleaDistributor and PleaLaunch in one transaction (launch.json). PleaLaunch then deploys the hook (CREATE2 at a flag-bearing address, salt mined off chain), the Letterbox and the Tribunal against keccak256 code hashes committed at launch, mints the fixed split (82% hook, 6% Seasons, 12% distributor) and seeds trading (the owner any time, anyone from START; on chain 1 seed checks the agreed timing and the 14,000 IMD market cap). PleaDistributor is a Merkle distributor whose root is set once.

Please look hardest at: Letters: escrow accounting (every PLEA and IMD it holds must belong to an open letter); the fee path's bounds; races between delivery, settle and expiry; the appeal's same-share clamp; reentrancy through the hook and the gate; anyone moving or locking someone else's escrow; text building. Token and launch: ways around the transfer restriction (approvals, transferFrom, the PoolManager, ERC-6909 claims, exempt senders); the one-shot init and the code-hash commitments; front-running a deploy or the seed; the mint split; the distributor's root and claims.

Please rate each finding by severity and give a concrete path: who calls what, in which state, and what they gain or what breaks. Gas notes are welcome but secondary.
