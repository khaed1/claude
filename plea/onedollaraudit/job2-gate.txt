Security review of PLEA run 8.5: CabalGate and OracleAttestation (pleas, scoring, oracle answers).

PLEA run 8.5, live on Sepolia as IMD launch #1981 (deployed 2026-10-16, not seeded yet). Source: public repo https://github.com/khaed1/plea at commit af57f6df709acdf7fe754a58482ee35eef9e2178 (branch testnet8.5); its src/ is byte-identical to the deployed launch source identity-md-launches/launch-1981-src-cabalgate-sol-src-tribunal-sol-src-p @ 2d4cf7a. Foundry, solc 0.8.26, via_ir, cancun; deps in lib/ (Uniswap v4-core, OpenZeppelin, solmate). This is the code we plan to launch on Ethereum mainnet; the mainnet build changes only settings (a 14,000 IMD opening market cap instead of 5,700, 15-day seasons instead of 5 hours, the oracle fee bought in IMD instead of minted from a test token).

What it is: a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4. Anyone can buy. Selling needs a plea: an oracle panel (IMD, 30 judges, quorum 17) scores the plea 0-45, and it passes when the score reaches a need of 71 minus an on-chain fact score (0-45). An approved plea is one sale, within 7 minutes, through the gate. 33 hours without a verdict kills the gate for good and PLEA then trades freely. Nine contracts in src/, 3,399 nSLOC.

Spec and the agreed invariants (latest): https://github.com/khaed1/plea/blob/54da76be64c6f01764e27e6cc5b65811475d5e77/audit/THREAT-MODEL.md (numbers in bold are design decisions, not findings). Every earlier finding and its status (latest): https://github.com/khaed1/plea/blob/54da76be64c6f01764e27e6cc5b65811475d5e77/audit/FINDINGS.md (eight rounds of AI-swarm audits plus IMD's launch audits; please do not re-report fixed or accepted items unless the fix itself is wrong). Two findings of run 8.5's own launch audit are known in this exact code and fixed after it: the share bands use the share floored to whole basis points (L85-M-1) and launch.json's seat-NFT address has a wrong letter case (L85-F-1). Tests: 823 (FOUNDRY_PROFILE=full forge test; the default profile builds only the launch suites).

What it does: The gate runs the plea lifecycle: submitSell and appeal (and the Letterbox's submitFor and appealFor), the fact score (share of the balance at most 20%: 16, at most 35%: 9, more: 3; held 7/3/1 days: 7/4/2; a profit table; the 24h trend from the hook's hourly checkpoints; a PLEA-weighted mean for PLEA no buy record covers), the 0.5 oracle fee paid to IMD's Intake, the oracle callbacks with an EIP-712 / ERC-1271 attestation check, the 7-minute approval window, cooldowns, a 20-minute expiry, the 33-hour dead-man, the approved sale through the hook (with an exact-output fee swap for letters), and a UTF-8 text validator that decodes code points and refuses control, format, separator, variation-selector and default-ignorable characters and marker prefixes. Trusted: the oracle signer, and the Intake's writer, which pairs an answer with its request (accepted risk L85-I-1 in FINDINGS).

Please look hardest at: Anything that lets a plea pass with a lower need, sell more or more often than approved, revive the Cabal or kill it early, replay or re-address an answer, block other sellers, or get the fee back; the attestation verification; the validator's UTF-8 decoding and its refused ranges; the fact score's edges and rounding.

Please rate each finding by severity and give a concrete path: who calls what, in which state, and what they gain or what breaks. Gas notes are welcome but secondary.
