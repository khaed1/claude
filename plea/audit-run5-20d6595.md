## What was checked and found correct

**Tribunal vs CabalGate parity.** `onOracleResult`: same `msg.sender == intake`, unknown-request, pending/expiry (`TooLate`), `_verifyAttestation` + `_consume`, `WrongPanel` (chain 1, panel ≥ 30, quorum ≥ 17, agreed ≥ 17 and ≥ quorum), `ScoreOutOfRange` (> 45), mapping delete before the state change. Failure function: same shape, void only while pending. `expire`/`_stale`: same two conditions (20 min, or `intake.requests(id).completed`), same `IIntake` getter the gate uses live. `_escape` byte-identical to the gate's; applied to the gate's whole plain question, which is exact because everything outside the two texts is ASCII from `Strings` and fixed literals (the suite parses the body with `vm.parseJson`, with quotes and backslashes in both texts). `DEFINITIONS`: the gate's five members verbatim (test compares each member of `gate.body` and `tribunal.body`), the `objection` member 488 characters (≤ 512). Text validator: the gate's, plus `[OBJECTION` / `[/OBJECTION` in any case (`_word`); the gate's own `[PLEA` rule keeps an objector from closing the plea block. Own EIP-712 domain (`verifyingContract` = the Tribunal in both the domain and the body's `consumer`), the gate's signer read at construction; cross-domain replay tested both ways. Re-entrancy: `object` and `settle` are `nonReentrant` (transient), all state is written before the token calls and the Intake call; `onOracleResult` makes only view calls to the gate; `settle` calls `Seasons.checkpoint` after the transfer and `checkpoint` is permissionless anyway. Stake accounting: `settle` pays each ruled or void objection once (`settled`), to the objector (sustained, void) or to Seasons (overruled); the fuzz keeps balance == unsettled stakes. Status changes mid-case: `gate.result` reports `approved` for `Approved`, `Executed` and `Lapsed`, so a case survives the sale or a lapse (tested). Last-second approval: window 20 + expiry 20 < grace 60, now proven on three paths (L4) including a void followed by an attempted second objection. `standing()`: sustained → never held; a pending objection holds only while not stale (by time or by completion); otherwise held iff approved, fewer than two rulings and inside the window. Sham/shield (spec "Known limits"): a seller's second wallet objecting first gambles the seller's own score (the min rule), spends a slot but leaves one, and a sustained sham erases the seller's points; a rival's sham costs 0.85 IMD per roll, two rolls at most, stake to the pot on an overrule. Griefing by repeated voids is bounded by the window (no new objection after +20 min) and the objector cannot force a void.

**Seasons.** `_standing` staticcall paths all fail open (now tested, L3); the EIP-150 1/64 rule makes it impossible to starve the staticcall and still afford `_record`, so a caller cannot forge "not held" with gas. `_tryRecord` inside `closeSeason` skips held or sustained pleas without reverting (tests) and the bound guarantees nothing of the closing season is still held at close time. `_insertWallet`: one storage slot per entry (address + uint16 + uint64 pack to 30 bytes), 25 cold reads plus the shifted writes; `closeSeason` worst case 25 IMD + 25 PLEA transfers, each failure-tolerant (`_pay`, raw `call`), well inside a block. `shareBps`: 450…350 (half-to-even rounding on the .5 steps), symmetric pairs sum to 800, total 10,000, strictly falling; `BONUS_PER_SEASON * shareBps / 10,000` is an integer for every rank, so a full season pays exactly 10,000,000; unpaid = 10M − paid, capped by the balance, burned. 6 × 10M = 60M = `PLEA.init`'s 6%. Owner / launch-owner exclusion unchanged from the fix round (`_owners`, `_dropOwner`, `_excluded`; the Tribunal re-uses it through `excluded(address)`).

**PleaLaunch.** `deployLetterbox` and `deployTribunal` both use CREATE2 salt 0 with different creation code, so different addresses; `predictTribunal` matches (manifest test); a zero `tribunalCodeHash` makes `deployTribunal` revert `TribunalOff` and `predictTribunal` return zero, and Seasons then records as before. Thirteen constructor arguments; `launch.json` lists them with both hashes; `test_launchJsonCommitsToThisTribunal` / `…Letterbox` tie the file to the build. Launch gas 13,115,297 as charged, 16,000,662 with 22% headroom (776,554 under 16,777,216); `deployTribunal` 4,794,732, `deployLetterbox` 5,381,041.

**PLEA.init and the numbers.** 82/6/12 = 820M / 60M / 120M (sums to 1e9); README, `launch.json` notes, the fork test (`60_000_000e18`), `Seasons`, the Letterbox leaderboard (25 ranks, `shareBps`) and every prize test agree; the job text lists the same figures.

**Letterbox after the hold.** `deliver` sells inside the 7-minute window and `_record` is a `try … catch`, so `Held` is swallowed and the points are recorded later by anyone (test); `settle` of a lapsed approval during a hold behaves the same; `_points`/`leaderboard` use 25 ranks and `shareBps`; the status line now shows the hold (L1).
