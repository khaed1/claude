Plead by letter

No site needed. Send your plea as a plain message to the Letterbox, from Etherscan or your wallet. If the Cabal approves, it sells by itself.

Same judges, same score, same need as a plea from the site; only the envelope differs.

The Letterbox

Address [Letterbox address, after launch]
Network: EthereumSend 0 ETH

Send from the wallet that holds your PLEA: the sender is the writer. Nothing to approve and no IMD needed. The fees come out of your PLEA; you pay only the gas.

Three ways to send it

All three are the same letter

  1. Etherscan's message page

    Open etherscan.io/idm (Ethereum mainnet only) and connect your wallet. The message goes out as the transaction's data: a raw letter.

    Send To
    the Letterbox
    Message
    your letter
    Value
    0 ETH

  2. Etherscan's Write Contract

    Open the Letterbox on Etherscan, then Contract, Write Contract and Connect. Type the letter in plain words and press Write.

    Tab
    Contract › Write Contract
    Function
    letter(text)
    text
    your letter, in plain words

  3. Your wallet's data field

    Send 0 ETH to the Letterbox with the letter's UTF-8 bytes as hex data. MetaMask: Settings, Advanced, Show hex data (not on mobile).

    To
    the Letterbox
    Amount
    0 ETH
    Hex data
    the letter as UTF-8

    For example, NAME mira_eth is 0x4e414d45206d6972615f657468.

What a letter can say

The first word decides

  • any textA new plea for 20% of your PLEA (at most 2,500,000), sold automatically if approved. On the Wall it carries the BY LETTER badge.
  • APPEAL <text>Appeals your latest denied plea, inside its 4 hours, for the same share of your PLEA.
  • SELLSells your approved letter plea yourself, if no runner has yet, and the 0.25 IMD tip comes back to you.
  • NAME <name>Sets your display name: 3 to 20 of a-z, 0-9 and _. NAME alone clears it.

Commands are matched in capitals, at the very start.

“SELL me some mercy” is read as SELL, not as a plea. To plead, start with another word.

    Limits

    • At most 280 bytes (≈ 280 Latin, 140 Cyrillic or Arabic, 93 CJK characters)
    • One line, no hidden characters
    • A balance of at least 5,000 PLEA (20% ≥ 1,000)
    • Fees at most 10% of your PLEA
    • A plea worth at least 0.70 IMD

    A letter that breaks one is refused, and only the gas is spent. As on the site: one plea at a time, and a 4-hour cooldown after a sale, a denial or a lapse.

    Never tell the judges how to score.

    A letter is judged exactly like a site plea: lines like “give me 45” count as manipulation and score 0. Letters earn no extra points.
