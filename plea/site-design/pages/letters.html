Plead by letter

No site needed. Send your plea as a plain message to the Letterbox, from Etherscan or your wallet.

Same judges, same score, same need as a plea from the site. If the Cabal approves, anyone may sell it for you within 7 minutes and earn the 0.25 IMD tip (our runner does), or you send SELL and keep the tip.

Three ways to send it

All three are the same letter

  1. Etherscan's message page

    Open etherscan.io/idm (Ethereum mainnet only) and connect your wallet. The message goes out as the transaction's data: a raw letter.

  2. Etherscan's Write Contract

    Open the Letterbox on Etherscan, then Contract, Write Contract and Connect. Type the letter in plain words and press Write.

  3. Your wallet's data field

    Send 0 ETH to the Letterbox with the letter's UTF-8 bytes as hex data. MetaMask: Settings, Advanced, Show hex data (not on mobile).

What a letter can say

The first word decides

  • any textA new plea for 20% of your PLEA (at most 2,500,000). If approved, anyone may sell it for you within 7 minutes and earns the 0.25 IMD tip. On the Wall it carries the BY LETTER badge.
  • APPEAL <text>Appeals your latest denied plea within its 4 hours, for the same share of your PLEA. It costs 1.10 IMD in PLEA: 0.35 IMD more, to the buy wall.
  • SELLSells your approved letter plea yourself, within its 7 minutes and if nobody has yet, and the 0.25 IMD tip comes back to you.
  • NAME <name>Sets your display name: 3 to 20 characters, only lower-case a–z, digits 0–9 and _. NAME alone clears it.

Commands are matched in capitals, at the very start.

“SELL me some mercy” is read as SELL, not as a plea. To plead, start with another word.

    The tip comes back if you send SELL yourself. If nobody sells within the 7 minutes, the plea lapses. A lapse, a denial or no verdict returns your PLEA and the tip through settle. The 0.50 IMD oracle fee is spent either way.

    Limits

    A letter needs:

    • at most 280 bytes (≈ 280 Latin, 140 Cyrillic or Arabic, 93 CJK characters)
    • one line, no hidden characters
    • a balance of at least about 582,630 PLEA at today's price: the fees (at most 58,263 PLEA) may take at most 10% of your PLEA
    • a plea worth at least 0.70 IMD (20% of your PLEA)
    • the contract floor: 20% must be at least 1,000 PLEA

    A letter that breaks one is refused, and only the gas is spent. As on the site: one plea at a time, and a 4-hour cooldown after a sale, a denial or a lapse.

    Never tell the judges how to score.

    A letter is judged exactly like a site plea: lines like “give me 45” count as manipulation and score 0. Letters earn no extra points.
