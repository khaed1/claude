    What happens next

    1. Submitted

      The contract checks your text and your need before it takes the fee.

    2. Waiting for the Cabal

      Up to 20 minutes, usually 3 to 6. 30 judges score it; 17 must agree.

    3. Verdict

      Approved: sell within 7 minutes. Objections stay open for 20 minutes, then your points are recorded.

      Denied: one appeal within 4 hours.

      No verdict (the panel split): cleared at once, no cooldown.

    4. Cooldown

      4 hours after a sale, a denial or a lapse.
