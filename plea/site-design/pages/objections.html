      How a ruling works

      A new panel of 30 re-scores the plea, from 0 to 45, against the same need.

      • Sustained

        The new score is more than 2 below the need: the plea gets no points, and your stake comes back.

      • Overruled

        Otherwise: the plea counts the lowest score any panel gave, and your stake goes to the season prize pot.

      • Void

        The panel splits or gives no answer within 20 minutes: nothing changes, and your stake comes back.
