How it works

Four steps, all on chain

  1. Buy

    Open to anyone. Every trade pays a 2.25% fee, and 0.5% comes back to you as staked IMD (0.49 IMD on a 100 IMD buy).

  2. Plead

    Write why you want to sell 1% to 50% of your PLEA (at most 2,500,000): 1 to 280 bytes, any language. Pay the 0.50 IMD oracle fee. The contract computes your need first.

  3. The Cabal judges

    30 judges score your plea from 0 to 45, and 17 must agree. It usually takes 3 to 6 minutes. A score at or above your need is approved.

  4. Sell in 7 minutes

    Approved: sell within 7 minutes, or tick auto-sell and anyone may sell it for you for a 0.25 IMD tip. Denied: one appeal within 4 hours. After a sale, a denial or a lapse: a 4-hour cooldown.

Your need

Before you pay, the contract scores four facts about your sale from the chain. Your need is 71 minus that fact score: the judges must reach it.

The fact score

0 to 45 points
Points for each fact
FactPoints
Share sold≤ 20%: 16 · ≤ 35%: 9 · more: 3
Held (buy-weighted)≥ 7 days: 7 · ≥ 3 days: 4 · ≥ 1 day: 2 · less: 0
Profit or lossat a loss: 13 · gain ≤ 50%: 12 · ≤ 200%: 10 · ≤ 500%: 8 · ≤ 1,000%: 6 · more: 4
24h trendup > 2%: 9 · flat ±2%: 5 · down: 0

PLEA that no buy record covers (received, not bought) counts as held since trading opened, with 10 points for profit or loss.

A need above 45 cannot pass.

The contract refuses such a plea before you pay (CannotPass), so no fee is spent. Plead a smaller share or wait for better facts.

Letters

Plead without the site: send your plea as text to the Letterbox from Etherscan. It pleads 20% of your PLEA and pays its fees in PLEA. If approved, anyone can sell it for you within 7 minutes and earns the 0.25 IMD tip; send SELL yourself and you keep it.

How to send a letter

Objection!

For 20 minutes after an approval, anyone else may object: 0.50 IMD fee + 0.35 IMD stake. A new panel of 30 re-scores the plea. The sale is never touched; only the points can fall.

Open objections

Seasons

15 days each; your best 3 scores count once you record them, after the objection window. The top 25 with 60+ points share the season prize pot in IMD, plus 10,000,000 PLEA in each of the first six seasons. A place below 60 points rolls its IMD into the next season and its PLEA is burned. IMD seat holders earn 1.2× points.

The owner's powers, in plain words

One switch, add-only. PLEA.allow(account) lets a sender move PLEA without a plea, permanently. Accounts can be added, never removed. It is for routers and the claim contract.

Nothing else. The owner cannot change the fees, the judges, the rules or anyone's pleas.

The owner does not play. Its pleas earn no points, no prize and no bonus.

While the Cabal lives you cannot sell without an approved plea.

Only buy what you are happy to hold.
