Share cards

One image per plea, 1200 × 675, drawn in the browser, for every state a plea can be in. The post links back to the plea's page. IPFS pages cannot make per-plea link previews, so the site offers Download image and Copy image, and the text under the post tells people to attach it.

  • 1200 × 675 PNG
  • Light; dark in the dark theme
  • Over 150 bytes: smaller text

One post shape for every state.

The state in capitals with the score against the need, then the plea's first two sentences in quotes (only the first when two would pass X's 280 characters), then “Plead your case:” and the link. Only the seller's own post says “my plea” (for mira_eth, #214); any other plea is named with its seller: “plea #213 by kafka_cat”.
