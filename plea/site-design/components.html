PLEA Design system · components

Components

Neo-brutal docket: cream paper, ink outlines, one action colour, one stamp. Every component below, in every state. Switch the theme with the moon button. Pages: mockups. Numbers come from sample-data.json.

Colour

Ink on paper, one action colour that is never a state, and one flat fill per verdict state with ink text on it in both themes. The stamp's word carries the state; pending is striped and lapsed is a dashed outline, so colour is never the only signal.

Space, borders, radii, shadows

One spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 px. Grid: 4, 8 and 12 columns at 360, 768 and 1280 px, max width 1200 px, gutters 16, 24 and 32 px. Outlines are 3 px (2 px on pills and badges). Hard shadows, never blurred, only on buttons, cards and the stamp.

Nav and status strip

Buttons

A button names its action. Primary (yellow, one per view), secondary (card), ink (the urgent one: sell inside the 7-minute window). Pressing sinks the button into its shadow. Busy shows the stripes and says what it waits for; disabled is dashed and says why next to it. All at least 44 px tall.

Inputs, the byte counter and the contract's text checks

Amounts are typed in mono with the unit inside the field. Every text box counts UTF-8 bytes (what the contract sees: 280 bytes is about 280 Latin, 140 Cyrillic or Arabic, 93 CJK characters) and runs the same checks as CabalGate._validateText, live, in text-check.js.         Pills and badges

        Pills hold live facts (status, season, price). Badges tag a plea or a wallet: the seat multiplier, a letter, auto-sell, you. State badges are the small stamp.

        The verdict stamp

        The signature. One shape everywhere: an ink-outlined box tilted 3°, the state in capitals, the score against the need or the time left under it. Sizes: small (tables, lists), medium (cards), large (the status page), extra large (the share card). It lands once when a verdict arrives live; with reduced motion it simply appears.

        Countdowns

        Mono digits, tabular. Ink with yellow digits only for the one that costs money if missed (the 7-minute sell window). Striped while waiting on the oracle. Minutes and seconds under an hour, hours and minutes under a day, days and hours above. Screen readers get a label, not a ticking number.

        Sell window 06:12 Waiting for the Cabal 12:30 Objections close 19:12 Appeal until 11:41 UTC 3h 41m Next plea 4h 00m Season 2 ends 9d 4h
        Sell within06:12The window closes at 08:06:12 UTC. Miss it and the plea lapses (the points still count).

        Tables and the leaderboard

        Numbers right-aligned in tabular figures. Your row is highlighted and tagged YOU. Seat holders show the badge and their raw score next to the 1.2× points. A bar marks the 60-point line: below it, no prize, the share rolls over. On phones each row folds into two lines; nothing scrolls sideways.
