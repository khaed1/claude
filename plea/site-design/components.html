The lilac dot means the wallet holds an IMD seat (1.2× points). A long wallet name ends in “…” (11 rem at most, 9 rem under 400 px) and shows in full on the profile; Connect and Switch to Ethereum are never cut.

Buttons

A button names its action. Primary (yellow, one per view), secondary (card), ink (the urgent one: sell inside the 7-minute window; yellow in dark). Pressing sinks the button into its shadow. Busy shows the stripes and says what it waits for, without moving; disabled is dashed and says why next to it (aria-disabled, so it stays focusable and ignores clicks). Labels wrap on small phones. All at least 44 px tall.

Inputs, the byte counter and the contract's text checks

Amounts are typed in mono with the unit inside the field.         Countdowns

        Mono digits, tabular. The urgent pair (black with yellow digits in light, yellow in dark, with the timer icon) only for the one that costs money if missed: the 7-minute sell window. Striped while waiting on the oracle. Minutes and seconds under an hour, hours and minutes under a day, days and hours above, except the dead-man, which always counts in hours (“32h 59m”, never “1d 8h”) because its window is 33 hours; the words say “in” or give the end time, so 19:12 never reads as a clock time. Screen readers get a label, not a ticking number.

        Tables and the leaderboard

        Numbers in tabular figures, mono values and scores never break. Your row is highlighted and tagged YOU. Every seat holder shows the badge and the raw scores next to the 1.2× points; each IMD amount is rounded down on its own, as the chain pays and prints it, so parts and totals can differ by a cent. A bar marks the 60-point line. On phones each row folds into three lines and every cell carries its own label (.lb__label); from 768 px the head row names the columns. Ranks 5 to 21, 24 and 25 are left out here; Seasons has all 25. The rulings table is .table--tight, with a .cell-note line under a cell; on phones it scrolls in its own box, which becomes a named tab stop only while it scrolls (data-label holds the name; site.js adds the rest).

        1. Buy

          Open to anyone. Every trade pays a 2.25% fee, and 0.5% comes back to you as staked IMD (0.49 IMD on a 100 IMD buy).

        2. Plead

          Write why you want to sell 1% to 50% of your PLEA (at most 2,500,000): 1 to 280 bytes, any language. Pay the 0.50 IMD oracle fee. The contract computes your need first.

        3. The Cabal judges

          30 judges score your plea from 0 to 45, and 17 must agree. It usually takes 3 to 6 minutes. A score at or above your need is approved.

        4. Sell in 7 minutes

          Approved: sell within 7 minutes, or tick auto-sell and anyone may sell it for you for a 0.25 IMD tip. Denied: one appeal within 4 hours. After a sale, a denial or a lapse: a 4-hour cooldown.
