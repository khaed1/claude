## The two projects
- **imd/acc:** trading cashback in staked IMD. Projects pay 0.25–1% of each trade (PLEA: 0.5%) out of their fees through the ownerless `Stacker.credit(trader, imd)`, which stakes it as sIMD for the trader. Listed projects earn points; listing is decided on chain by the ownerless **ProjectRegistry** (anyone applies, an IMD oracle panel of 15 answers yes/no, 11 must agree). Sepolia test run 2 (launch #1294): Stacker `0x0457276d7b036818b3eddc1a4a3fceda61e9314d`, ProjectRegistry `0x3a3bfcb65614292b0ec7c62f9e3a5dac003b920e`, site https://imd-acc-sepolia-test-run-2.sites.imd.fun/ (Apply page `#/apply`).
- **PLEA:** a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4. Buying is open; **selling needs a plea** judged by "the Cabal" (IMD oracle panel, 30 judges, quorum 17, score 0–45); pass if score ≥ need = 70 − on-chain fact score (0–55). Approved → sell within 7 min; denied → one appeal within 4h; 4h cooldown after a sale, denial or lapse. One hook (fork of POOL4's CappedBurnHook): fees 1.75% (0.5% sIMD cashback, 0.5% owner, 0.25% prize pot, 0.25% locked liquidity, 0.25% PLEA burn); every buy needs `abi.encode(buyer)` as hookData. The Cabal dies after 33h without a signed verdict, then trading is free forever. Seasons back to back (5h on Sepolia, 15 days on mainnet): best 3 scores count, top 10 with ≥60 points get 25/18/14/11/9/7/6/4/3/3 % of the IMD pot, top 5 pleas become Laureate NFTs. **Run 2 adds:** a 5,000,000 PLEA bonus for each of the first 4 seasons (supply 88% hook / 2% Seasons / 10% distributor; unpaid burned), Intake v2 failure clearing (`onPleaFailure`), 25-day cap decay, wall guard; IMD's audit fixed the wall guard (ignores empty-pool ticks), the hourly checkpoint (pre-swap price) and made the Stacker approval per-credit.
