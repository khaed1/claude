The six contracts

None has an owner, a pause, an upgrade path or a setter for any parameter. Every state-changing function is guarded against re-entry. They call three Poseidon hash contracts that already exist on mainnet. A one-time deployer creates five of them and then has no function left, and the Distribution contract creates the token at launch.

1. Pool

Holds every asset. Takes deposits, activates them, verifies spends, keeps the note tree, the spent set, the caps and the reward index.

The reward index. Advanced before any change to a shielded balance, and at every roll:

g        += seconds × emission(step) × 2^64 / max(weighted, floor(step))     // per cap step crossed, rounded down
released += seconds × emission(step) × weighted / max(weighted, floor(step)) // rounded up, and never above the users' 45%
weighted  = Σ over ETH, USDT, USDC and IMD of weight[asset] × shielded[asset]
floor     = a quarter of Σ weight[asset] × cap(asset) at the current step
emission  = the users' 45%, spread over eight cap steps in proportion to each step's cap; zero afterwards

Invariants.

  1. For each capped asset, the pool's balance is at least pending + shielded + fees. For the reward token it is at least pending + fees + tokenIn − tokenOut plus the reward budget not yet withdrawn.
  2. A spend can never take out more of a capped asset than shielded, and never more of the reward token than tokenIn + released − tokenOut.
  3. shielded[asset] never exceeds the asset's cap at the time it last grew. Caps never fall.
  4. Only its depositor can activate a deposit. A refund goes to the depositor whoever calls it. Each deposit ends in exactly one of the two.
  5. A nullifier is marked once and never cleared. The tree only grows, by one leaf per activation or spend.
  6. g never decreases and stays under 290. released never exceeds the users' 45%. gCur and gPrev are never above g.
  7. After the deposit window closes no deposit is created. Activation, refund, roll and spend have no end date.
  8. No function sends an asset anywhere except: a refund to its depositor, a withdrawal to the recipient and a relay fee to the relayer named in a verified proof, and fees to the distributor.
  9. No proof is accepted before the Verifier exists: a call to an address without code counts as a failed verification.

2. Verifier

The Groth16 verifier for Spend, generated from the ceremony's output. One view function, verify(proof, inputs[13]). No storage. Its invariant is that it accepts exactly the proofs the verification key accepts, which is tested against the reference verifier with valid, altered and malformed proofs, including public inputs at or above the field modulus. A separate script checks the key itself: gamma and delta distinct, every point on the curve, 14 input points.

3. ScreenerRegistry

Decides which deposits may be activated. Holds screener stakes, the screeners' share of fees and the screeners' 15% of the token. It cannot reach anything in the pool. If it fails, deposits stop and nothing already shielded is affected.

Batch {                   // hashed as keccak256(abi.encode(...)) of the fields in this order
  uint32  epoch;
  bytes32 criteriaHash;   // the published criteria file in force
  uint64  upTo;           // highest deposit id that was at least one day old at the epoch's start
  Word[]  words;          // { index, mask }, ascending: the full refusal bitmap of each word that differs from the registry's at the epoch's start
  bytes32 dataCid;        // CIDv1, raw, sha2-256, of a canonical JSON file: every refusal and the rule that caused it
}
// If more than 96 words differ, the batch lists the first 96 and lowers upTo to just below the first deposit of the first word left out.

What a miss is. When an epoch ends, the lead hash is recorded as that epoch's majority hash if more than half of the snapshot revealed it. An accepted batch is always the majority hash. A screener that revealed something else in such an epoch has one more miss, and a screener that revealed the majority hash has none. Seven in a row allow eviction. This is what stops a minority from blocking deposits for ever by revealing junk, and it also means a bloc that holds a majority for a week can remove everyone else.

Pay. At the first registry call after an epoch with an accepted batch, the pot and one share of the 15% are divided equally among the screeners who revealed that batch, and written to the ring. The 15% is paid in 730 shares, one per accepted epoch, so a day without a batch delays its share and does not destroy it. An epoch with no accepted batch pays nobody and its fees stay in the pot. A screener is credited at its next commit, exit, eviction or withdrawal, within 16 epochs.

After deposits close. The registry takes no commits from 30 epochs after the deposit window closes. From then on the distributor sends the screeners' 40% of fees to stakers.

Events. Registered, ScreenerActive, Committed, Revealed, BatchAccepted(epoch, batchHash, agreed), MajorityRecorded(epoch, hash), BatchApplied(epoch, upTo, dataTime), Flag(seatId, data), CriteriaSignal(seatId, hash), ExitRequested, Evicted(seatId, reason), PayWithdrawn.

Invariants.

  1. A batch is accepted only with matching reveals from at least two thirds of the snapshot, rounded up, and only when the snapshot is at least seven. At most one batch is accepted per epoch.
  2. Only apply writes dataTime, screenedThrough or denied, only with a batch that hashes to the accepted batch of an epoch later than the last applied.
  3. No deposit is approved unless it was at least one day old at the start of the epoch of the last applied batch. No batch, however it was produced, can approve a deposit made after that time.
  4. A screener commits and reveals at most once per epoch, only while active, and only while the registered holder still owns the seat.
  5. A stake leaves only to the holder who posted it, 14 epochs after exit or eviction. Nothing can reduce it.
  6. For each asset, the registry's balance is at least the pot plus everything owed plus, for the token, the stakes, the unbonding stakes and the unpaid part of the 15%. Total token pay never exceeds the 15%.
  7. A ring slot is read only when its tag matches the epoch asked for.
  8. The registry has no function that moves funds held by the pool.

4. FeeDistributor

Receives the pool's fees and splits each receipt 40, 40 and 20. Holds staked tokens. Sells the 20% for IMD and spends the IMD in the locked pool.

Invariants.

  1. Every receipt is split 40, 40 and 20, with any rounding remainder going to the basket stream.
  2. For each asset, the balance is at least the unpaid staker stream plus everything owed plus the basket and its stream, plus the staked tokens for the token and buybackImd for IMD.
  3. Staked tokens return only to the staker who staked them.
  4. buybackImd falls only through a swap in the locked pool, and no function sends it to an address. IMD owed to stakers and screeners is accounted apart from it.
  5. A buyback step never exceeds half of the pool's fee rate times the locked position's virtual IMD reserve, and at most one step runs per 12 hours, so the IMD spent in a day never exceeds the size at which a sandwich starts to pay.
  6. A buyer receives only assets it named, and only their released basket balance.

5. Distribution

Creates the token, gives the supply out once, and owns the locked liquidity position.

FunctionWhoWhat it does
launch()Anyone, once, from GENESISCreates the Token with CREATE2 and a salt taken from the block's prevrandao and the caller, so its address does not exist and cannot be known before this block. Sends 45% to the pool and 15% to the registry. Creates the token and IMD pool at the 1% fee tier and sets the starting price. If someone in the same block has already created it at a higher price, sells at most a small bounded number of tokens param with the starting price as the limit, sends the IMD received to the distributor with fundBuyback, and reverts the whole call if the price is still above the start. Then adds what is left of the 25% as one position from the starting price to the top of the tick range, held by this contract, and records the token, the pool and the position's liquidity
token(), pool(), liquidity()ViewsRead by the other contracts. Zero before launch
claimFounder()AnyoneSends the vested part of 5% to the founder address fixed in the build. Vested is zero for the first 30 days after launch, then 5% times the time since launch over 730 days, so the first month's share arrives at the end of the cliff
claimSwarm(index, account, amount, proof)AnyoneChecks the entry against the Merkle root fixed in the build, with leaves keccak256(bytes.concat(keccak256(abi.encode(index, account, amount)))), and sends the vested part to account. Linear from launch over the swarm period param

Storage. token, pool, liquidity, launchTime, founderClaimed, swarmClaimed[index]. Events. Launched(token, pool, liquidity), FounderClaimed(amount), SwarmClaimed(index, account, amount), FeesCollected(imd, tokenBurned).

Invariants.

  1. launch runs once. Afterwards the five shares add up to the whole supply, less at most the bounded number of tokens sold in a price reset.
  2. No code path reduces the position's liquidity. The contract never calls the pool's burn with a non-zero amount.
  3. The founder's and each swarm entry's payout never exceeds the vested amount, and never exceeds the allocation.
  4. The position's fees can go only to the distributor and the burn address.
  5. The gas launch can use is bounded by what one block's worth of interference can add, and stays under the transaction limit.

6. Token

A plain ERC-20 with 18 decimals, created by the Distribution contract at launch. The whole supply param is created for its creator in the constructor. There is no owner, no mint, no burn function, no fee on transfer and no blocklist. Its invariants are that total supply never changes and that no address has any power another does not.
