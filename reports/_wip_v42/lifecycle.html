How a payment moves

sequenceDiagram
  participant U as Wallet
  participant P as Pool contract
  participant S as Screeners
  participant B as Relayer
  U->>P: Deposit from a public address, the amount plus 0.2% (nothing is shielded yet)
  S->>S: Each day, commit and then reveal the batch of covered and refused deposits
  S->>P: Batch accepted at two thirds and applied
  U->>P: Activate after about two days (the note enters the tree)
  U->>B: Private transfer or withdrawal, encrypted
  B->>P: Submit and pay gas
  P-->>B: Relay fee in ETH, from the user's shielded fee note
  P-->>U: New shielded notes, reward carried inside them
  P-->>U: Withdrawal in a standard size, minus exactly 0.2%
  1. Deposit. You send the amount you want shielded plus 0.2%, from a public address, together with a hidden commitment to the key that will own it. The deposit is public. Nothing is shielded yet.
  2. Screening. Once a day the screeners agree on a batch. It covers every deposit that was at least a day old when the day began, and says which of them are refused. A batch needs two thirds of the active screeners.
  3. Activation. When a batch covers your deposit without refusing it, you activate it from the same address, and a note of exactly the amount enters the tree. From deposit to activation takes about two days: at least one day and sixteen hours, at most two days and sixteen hours, when screeners reveal on time.
  4. Refund. At any time before activation you can take the deposit and its 0.2% back, to the address it came from. No screening decision can stop it. A refused deposit can also be sent back by anyone.
  5. Private use. An activated note is an ordinary shielded note. You can hold it, split it and pay other users. Observers see that a transaction happened and nothing about who, how much or which asset.
  6. Relaying. Your wallet hands the encrypted transaction to a relayer picked at random, so your own address never pays the gas. The relayer is paid in ETH out of a small shielded ETH note you keep for the purpose. The fee is public, like a gas payment, and says nothing about what was moved.
  7. Withdrawal. Funds leave to any address in standard sizes. The recipient receives the size minus 0.2%, the same amount for everyone who withdraws that size. A larger amount leaves as several standard withdrawals, spread over time by the wallet.
