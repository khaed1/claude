Parameters fixed in the build

Whatever values are deployed can never be changed. Rows marked decided are the owner's decisions. Rows marked to confirm are recommendations from Defaults to confirm. Rows marked build are engineering constants the build jobs may tune before the ceremony, with the reason recorded. Every duration is in seconds of block time.
