Independent review of revision 4.1

Revision 4.1 was reviewed and redesigned by one author. On 11 November 2026 a separate reviewer, given only the published page and told to break it, read all of it. It found one critical fault, six high ones, 24 places where sections contradicted each other and 28 points a builder would have had to guess. It also checked 20 of the earlier claims and found them correct, among them that total reward paid can never exceed the emission. Its full note is adversarial_review_independent_v3.md in the repository. Revision 4.2 is the answer to it.

Other changes that came out of it

  • The relayer is paid in ETH. A fee paid as a shielded note cost the relayer about one more transaction to collect, so users would have paid twice the gas, and the relayer learned the asset. A small shielded ETH note now pays a public fee.
  • The entry fee is paid on top. A 1 ETH deposit used to become a 0.998 ETH note, which left in seven withdrawals and stranded 0.003 ETH.
  • Schedules count seconds. Block counts would have halved every wait if Ethereum shortens its slots.
  • Exits cannot be blocked by filling the root window with empty spends: the root saved at each checkpoint is accepted too.
  • The 20% enters the basket evenly over a week, so a lump of fees is not sold at a stale price.
  • Refused deposits can be sent back by anyone, and the screeners' 40% goes to stakers when there is nothing to screen.
  • The deployer no longer depends on its own output, the canary's caps are small enough to fill, and the launch gate says plainly that nothing on-chain enforces it.

Claims in revision 4.1 that were wrong

  • "Commit and reveal so that copying is not possible." It stops copying from the chain only.
  • "Individual reveals so nobody can be left out." A two-thirds bloc can still agree on a batch the others did not compute.
  • Blocking "earns the blockers nothing". It earned them the rewards if they were already inside.
  • "The pool recovers from ordinary attrition by itself." Someone has to call evict.
  • Capture means "a bad deposit approved". It means control of entry.
  • "One to two days for screeners." It was zero to one.
  • "The launch code handles a pool that already exists." It could be blocked.
  • "That IMD stays in the pool as bids for good." Only for the locked position's share of the liquidity in range.
  • The canary "calls every function and every branch". Its full-cap line could not be reached as funded.

Not adopted

  • Refusals that expire unless renewed, to make refusing everyone costly. A bloc that controls the registry can renew them.
  • A launch that never reverts, selling into any bids it finds. Unbounded, it would hand the whole public allocation to whoever parked bids just above the start.
  • A third ciphertext for the relayer. Paying the relayer in ETH removes the need.

Revision 4.2 has not itself been reviewed. The pattern of the last two days is that each review finds faults in the fixes for the last one, so no build job should be submitted before a third reviewer has read this page.
