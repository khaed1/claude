Each row gives the value in the first version 4, what this revision recommends, and why. The build specification is written to the recommended value and marks it as a parameter, so a different choice changes a constant and not the design, except where the last column says otherwise.

Where this revision disagrees with a decision

The owner's decisions are built as decided. Three notes are recorded here because the evidence supports them, and none is acted on.

  • Stakers are paid in the asset the fee was taken in, as decided. Selling the whole fee for IMD in the one auction and paying stakers and screeners in IMD would remove most of the accounting in the distributor and the registry, and would tie the project more closely to IMD. It would also expose all fee income to IMD's price. It is an option, not a recommendation.
  • The canary places a public token pool. A canary that exercises the token contracts has to launch a token. The decision to rely on a canary therefore brings the lawyer's review forward to before the canary.
  • DAI was left out. It is the least used of the three stablecoins and the only one no issuer can freeze. With USDT and USDC only, every dollar in the pool can be frozen by its issuer. That is the owner's choice and keeps the exposure at four capped assets.
