What was checked for this revision

Seven assumptions that version 4 rested on were checked on 10 November 2026 against source code, chain data and primary pages. The notes, with quotes and links, are in the repository under research_notes/ with names ending _v3.md. Nothing was built, deployed or sent to anyone.

Can IMD's launch factory issue a token with no owner and no mint, and send its trade fee to a contract?Verified Yes to both. It deploys whatever token code it is given, and none of the 431 tokens it has launched has a mint function. The "1%" is 80% of a 1.25% Uniswap v4 pool fee, paid to an address that may be a contractIt still does not fit. Only IMD's own deployer key can call it. It always gives 10% of supply to seat holders. Has IMD audited a zero-knowledge circuit, and what does its audit template cover?Verified No circuit audit was found among 432 to 450 public audits. The template is a Solidity checklist adapted from Pashov's solidity-auditor: four specialists (math, permissions, economics, control flow) and a judge. It does not mention circuits, Groth16 or trusted setups. IMD's verifier builds and tests Foundry projects onlyContract audits can use the template. The circuit cannot. The build plan gives the circuit its own checklist review and puts the weight on machine-checked proofs. Circuit and proof jobs are accepted by IMD on file structure alone, so each is re-run by a second job

Inside a spend they pay 24 gas a byte as calldata plus log: about 29,000 gas for the 1,216-byte recipient ciphertext, the same under Glamsterdam. Found along the way

  • IMD's token can be inflated by its owner. The IMD token on Ethereum is a bridged token whose owner, a single key, sets the bridge peers that are allowed to mint. It has no transfer tax, blocklist or pause. Its main market is an unlocked Uniswap v4 pool of about $1.14M. The public pool and the buyback are both priced in IMD.
  • Seats are concentrated. 2,000 seats sit at 791 addresses. The largest holds 77, the top five 243, the top ten 382, and 38 addresses hold ten or more. One address could register more screeners than a launch set of 15 independent ones needs to be outvoted two to one.
  • Anyone can pre-create the token's Uniswap pool. Uniswap v3's factory does not check that a token exists, and a pool's first price is set by whoever calls first. Checked in the v3 core source.
  • The sandwich bound. A buyback step no larger than the pool's fee rate times its IMD reserves cannot be sandwiched at a profit. Checked numerically in reports/sim/buyback_sandwich_bound.py.
  • Setup mistakes have been exploited. In February 2026 two live Groth16 verifiers were drained because a setup step was skipped. The build plan checks the verification key explicitly.
  • FinCEN withdrew its 2020 recordkeeping and travel-rule proposal on 6 November, the third digital-asset proposal withdrawn in five weeks. It concerns financial institutions, not pools.

  • How many people stand behind the 791 seat addresses.
  • Whether Uniswap governance takes, or can start taking, a share of trading fees on a v3 pool like this one.
  • IMD's budget per job step, and posts by its developer on X or Telegram.
  • The changes this revision makes to the design. They answer the review, and they have been read by nobody but their author. The review jobs in the build plan are the first outside look.
