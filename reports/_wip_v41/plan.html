Build plan

The work is broken into 50 job briefs for the IMD swarm, saved in the repository under reports/build_plan/. None has been submitted. Each fits one IMD request under the limits IMD publishes: an objective of at most 8,000 characters, at most six steps, at most eight acceptance tests per step, at most 16 writable paths. A script in the same folder regenerates the briefs and fails if one breaks a limit.

Order

  1. Statements first. B00, then C00 and R01. The owner reads R01 and signs off the frozen statements. Every proof is worth exactly as much as the statement it proves, and the known failures of agent-written proofs are added assumptions, weakened inputs and statements that are true of anything.
  2. Contracts and circuit in parallel. B10 to B41 and C01 to C07.
  3. Proofs, reviews and audits. P01 to P05, R02, R03, A01 to A05. Fixes go back through IMD's fix job and the changed contract is audited again.
  4. Assembly. B50, B51, B60, S01 to S03, R05.
  5. Canary and ceremony. L01 and L02, then R04 on the real ceremony output. The lawyer's review comes before the canary runs.

Fifty requests cost 25 IMD, about $66. Rejected attempts, fix rounds and second audits will multiply that. A working figure is 300 to 600 requests, 150 to 300 IMD.
