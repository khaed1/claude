Economics, simulated

These tables are arithmetic on the design, not forecasts. The script is reports/sim/murmuration_economics.py in the repository, with its full output in results.md beside it. Prices are those of 10 November 2026: ETH at $2,130 and IMD at $2.64. Supply is set to one billion tokens for readable numbers. Every result in IMD or in percent of supply holds for any supply.

User reward per unit of shielded value

Emissions follow the caps, so a full pool pays the same number of tokens per ETH per day in every period: 261 tokens, of a supply of one billion. In dollars that depends on the token's price.

  • 0.39% of the users' tokens are paid in the first 90 days, 5.9% in the first year and 50.2% in the last 90 days.
  • Under the first version 4 rule, emission divided by the total shielded with no floor, a single depositor of 0.001 ETH in an empty pool would have collected the whole first period, a nominal 42,578% a year. With the floor the rate never exceeds four times the full-pool rate.
  • With the recommended stop at 800 ETH, emissions level off with the caps: 525 tokens per ETH per day on a full pool, 1.14% a year at the starting price, and the last three periods each pay a quarter of the users' tokens.
