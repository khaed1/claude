How a payment moves

sequenceDiagram
  participant U as Wallet
  participant P as Pool contract
  participant S as Screeners
  participant B as Relayer
  U->>P: Deposit from a public address (nothing is shielded yet)
  S->>S: Each day, commit and then reveal the batch of covered and refused deposits
  S->>P: Batch accepted at two thirds and applied
  U->>P: Activate after at least one day (0.2% taken, note enters the tree)
  U->>B: Private transfer or withdrawal, encrypted
  B->>P: Submit and pay gas
  P-->>U: New shielded notes, reward carried inside them
  P-->>U: Withdrawal in a standard size, minus exactly 0.2%
  1. Deposit. You send funds from a public address together with a hidden commitment to the key that will own them. The deposit is public. Nothing is shielded yet and no fee is taken.
  2. Screening. Once a day the screeners agree on a batch: every deposit made before the day's first block, and which of them are refused. A batch needs two thirds of the active screeners.
  3. Activation. When your deposit is covered, not refused and at least 7,200 blocks old, you activate it from the same address. The pool takes the 0.2% entry fee and adds your note to the tree. From deposit to activation takes one to two days.
  4. Refund. At any time before activation you can take the deposit back in full, to the address it came from. Nobody else can touch it, and no screening decision can stop it.
  5. Private use. An activated note is an ordinary shielded note. You can hold it, split it and pay other users. Observers see that a transaction happened and nothing about who, how much or which asset.
  6. Relaying. Your wallet hands the encrypted transaction to a relayer picked at random, so your own address never pays the gas. The relayer is paid with a shielded note inside the same proof.
  7. Withdrawal. Funds leave to any address in standard sizes. The recipient receives the size minus 0.2%, the same amount for everyone who withdraws that size. A larger amount leaves as several standard withdrawals, spread over time by the wallet.
