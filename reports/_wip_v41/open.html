Open questions

  1. Will the owner confirm the ten values in Defaults to confirm? Three of them change the design: the fallback, the stop on the caps, and the asset list.
  2. Can agents produce proofs of this circuit that stand up? The tooling and one precedent exist. Nothing in the research shows IMD has done it. This launch is the test.
  3. Who signs off the formal statements? The plan gives that to the owner after a panel review. It is the one step that cannot be delegated to the checker.
  4. Who coordinates the trusted-setup ceremony, given that the team operates nothing?
  5. Who writes and maintains the criteria file and the lists it pins, and will enough screeners post flags for fresh thefts to be caught inside the window?
  6. Is a registry that one large seat holder could dominate acceptable to the wallets and exchanges the pool needs?
  7. Which wallet will ship the pool? Nobody has been asked.
  8. Will exchanges accept screening at entry, as Railgun does it, or expect screening at exit, as Privacy Pools does it?
  9. Will any wallet with real users fill its trusted-signer list from seat delegations? Until one does, the mesh relays only for the reference wallet.
  10. Who operates the public PPOI node at ppoi.fdi.network, and on what terms may a third-party broadcaster rely on it?
  11. Do Railway's iOS and Android builds enforce the allowlist? The web and desktop minimum version does. The mobile minimum predates the change.
  12. Who operates the four allowlisted fee signers and the three bootstrap nodes of Railgun's Waku cluster?
  13. How will Judge Failla's Rule 29 ruling and the April 2027 retrial treat independent relayers? The government's position at the April 2026 hearing was that relayers are part of the operating cohort.
  14. Does an EU seat holder who relays or screens for a fee fall under any regulated category? AMLR Article 2(1)(25) defines anonymity-enhancing coins and includes optional privacy. Recital 160 carves out software providers without access to wallets. AMLA's guidance on self-hosted addresses is due by 10 July 2027.
  15. Will EIP-8037 and EIP-8038 keep today's numbers after the developer call of 19 November 2026?
  16. Does Uniswap governance take, or plan to take, a share of trading fees on v3 pools at the 1% tier?
  17. Does Railgun adopt frame transactions or a 4337 path first, and remove the relayer before the mesh matters?
