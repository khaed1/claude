Phase 212 to 24 months

Proofs, canary, ceremony and launch of the pool

  • The 50 jobs of the build plan: contracts, the circuit, machine-checked proofs, reviews and IMD audits.
  • A lawyer's review of the fee, the token, the public pool and the founder allocation, before the canary.
  • A public trusted-setup ceremony for the one circuit, and a check of its output.
  • The canary: the whole system on mainnet for 90 days with a compressed clock, funded with at least 10 ETH and exercised by a published script.
  • Deployment of the six contracts through the deterministic deployment proxy and the one-time deployer, and placement of the public pool against IMD.
  • The wallet module ships the pool beside Railgun and Privacy Pools.

The pool launches only when all six conditions hold. There is no date.

  1. Every IMD audit and review finding is public and closed, for the six contracts and for the circuit.
  2. The circuit has machine-checked proofs of the five properties against statements the owner has signed off, the proved constraint system is the one in the ceremony, and public analyser runs are clean.
  3. A lawyer has reviewed the fee, the token, the public pool and the founder allocation. This comes before the canary, which places a token pool of its own.
  4. The canary has run 90 days on mainnet without loss, and every line of its script has a transaction hash.
  5. Three months on testnet, with at least 15 screeners on distinct seats producing matching batches for 30 days.
  6. A wallet with real users ships the pool.

Dependencies IMD's audit quality, which this launch tests · the clean proof framework and its Poseidon proofs · Glamsterdam and Hegotá dates · the Storm retrial in April 2027 and the EU rules that apply from 10 July 2027
