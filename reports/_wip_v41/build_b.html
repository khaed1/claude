The six contracts

None has an owner, a pause, an upgrade path or a setter for any parameter. Every state-changing function is guarded against re-entry. They call three Poseidon hash contracts that already exist on mainnet, and a one-time deployer creates them and then has no function left.

1. Pool

Holds every asset. Takes deposits, activates them, verifies spends, keeps the note tree, the spent set, the caps and the reward index.

The reward index. On every activation and every spend with a public amount, before any balance changes:

g        += blocks × emission(step) × 2^64 / max(weighted, floor(step))     // per cap step crossed, rounded down
released += blocks × emission(step) × weighted / max(weighted, floor(step)) // rounded up
weighted  = Σ over assets of weight[asset] × shielded[asset]
floor     = a quarter of the weighted total the pool can hold at the current caps
emission  = the users' 45%, spread over eight cap steps in proportion to each step's cap; zero afterwards

Invariants.

  1. For each asset, the pool's balance is at least pending + shielded + fees. For the reward token it is at least pending + fees + tokenIn − tokenOut plus the reward budget not yet withdrawn.
  2. A spend can never take out more of an asset than was activated into it, and never more of the reward token than was activated plus released.
  3. shielded[asset] never exceeds the asset's cap at the block it last grew. Caps never fall.
  4. Only its depositor can activate or refund a deposit, and each deposit ends in exactly one of the two.
  5. A nullifier is marked once and never cleared. The tree only grows, by one leaf per activation or spend.
  6. g never decreases and stays under 290. released never exceeds the users' 45%.
  7. After the deposit window closes no deposit is created. Activation, refund and spend have no end date.
  8. No function sends an asset anywhere except: a refund to its depositor, a withdrawal to the recipient named in a verified proof, and fees to the distributor.

2. Verifier

The Groth16 verifier for Spend, generated from the ceremony's output. One view function, verify(proof, inputs[11]). No storage. Its invariant is that it accepts exactly the proofs the verification key accepts, which is tested against the reference verifier with valid, altered and malformed proofs, including public inputs at or above the field modulus.

3. ScreenerRegistry

Decides which deposits may be activated. Holds screener stakes, the screeners' share of fees and the screeners' 15% of the token. It cannot reach anything in the pool. If it fails, deposits stop and nothing already shielded is affected.

  uint32  epoch;
  bytes32 criteriaHash;   // the published criteria file in force
  uint64  upTo;           // highest deposit id made before the epoch's first block
  Word[]  words;          // { index, mask }: full refusal bitmap for each word that differs from what is on-chain
  bytes32 dataCid;        // IPFS file: every refusal and the rule that caused it
}

Pay. When an epoch ends with an accepted batch, the pot and that epoch's share of the 15% are divided equally among the screeners who revealed that batch. An epoch with no accepted batch pays nobody: its token share stays locked for good and its fees roll to the next accepted epoch.

Events. Registered, Committed, Revealed, BatchAccepted(epoch, batchHash, agreed), BatchApplied(epoch, upTo), Flag(seatId, data), ExitRequested, Evicted, PayWithdrawn.

Invariants.

  1. A batch is accepted only with matching reveals from at least two thirds of the screeners active at the epoch's start, and only when at least seven were active.
  2. Only apply writes screenedThrough or denied, only with a batch that hashes to an accepted one, and screenedThrough never falls.
  3. A screener commits and reveals at most once per epoch, and only while the registered holder still owns the seat.
  4. A stake leaves only to the holder who posted it, and only 14 epochs after exit or eviction. Nothing can reduce it.
  5. For each asset, the registry's balance is at least the pot plus everything owed plus, for the token, the stakes and the unpaid part of the 15%.
  6. The registry has no function that moves funds held by the pool.

4. FeeDistributor

Receives the pool's fees and splits each receipt 40, 40 and 20. Holds staked tokens. Sells the liquidity share for IMD and spends the IMD in the locked pool.

Events. Notified(asset, toStakers, toScreeners, toBasket), Staked, Unstaked, Claimed, BasketSold(saleId, buyer, price), Buyback(imdIn, tokensBurned).

Invariants.

  1. Every receipt is split 40, 40 and 20, with any rounding remainder going to the basket.
  2. For each asset, the balance is at least the unpaid stream plus everything owed plus the basket.
  3. Staked tokens return only to the staker who staked them.
  4. IMD leaves only through a swap in the locked pool. No function sends it to an address.
  5. A buyback step never exceeds half of the pool's fee rate times its IMD-side reserves, and at most one step runs per 25 blocks.
  6. A basket asset whose transfer fails cannot block the sale of the others.

5. Distribution

Receives the whole supply at creation and gives it out once. Owns the locked liquidity position.

FunctionWhoWhat it does
launch()Anyone, onceSends 45% to the pool and 15% to the registry. Creates the token and IMD pool at the 1% fee tier if it does not exist and sets the starting price if it is not set. If someone has already set a higher price, sells a bounded number of tokens to bring it down to the start, and reverts if that is not enough. Then adds 25% of supply as one position from the starting price to the top of the tick range, held by this contract
claimFounder()AnyoneSends the vested part of 5% to the founder address fixed at creation: nothing for the first month, then linear to two years
claimSwarm(index, account, amount, proof)AnyoneChecks the entry against the Merkle root fixed at creation and sends the vested part to account. Linear from launch over the swarm period param
collect()AnyoneCollects the position's trading fees. The IMD goes to the distributor's buyback balance. The token part is burned

Storage. launched, founderClaimed, swarmClaimed[index]. Events. Launched(pool, liquidity), FounderClaimed(amount), SwarmClaimed(index, account, amount), FeesCollected(imd, tokenBurned).

Invariants.

  1. launch runs once, and afterwards the five shares add up to the whole supply.
  2. No code path reduces the position's liquidity. The contract never calls the pool's burn with a non-zero amount.
  3. The founder's and each swarm entry's payout never exceeds the vested amount, and never exceeds the allocation.
  4. The position's fees can go only to the distributor and the burn address.

6. Token

A plain ERC-20 with 18 decimals. The whole supply param is created for the Distribution contract in the constructor. There is no owner, no mint, no burn function, no fee on transfer and no blocklist. Its invariants are that total supply never changes and that no address has any power another does not.
