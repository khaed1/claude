# Murmuration v4 economics: simulation results

Generated by `reports/sim/murmuration_economics.py`. Prices of 10 November 2026: ETH $2,130, IMD $2.64. Supply assumed 1,000,000,000 tokens for readable numbers. This is arithmetic on the design, not a forecast.
