Scope note: covers Monero, Zcash, Dash, Firo, Grin/Beam, Penumbra, Namada, Aleo, Secret Network, Oasis Sapphire, newcomers (Midnight), the regulatory picture, and which ideas transfer to an Ethereum-based design. ## Key Question 1: Monero — mechanism, FCMP++ status, known deanonymization, delistings, 2025–2026 trend

### Takeaway
Monero remains the largest mandatory-privacy coin (~$11B market cap, XMR ≈ $590–610 in late Oct/early Nov 2026, ATH $797.73 on 14 Jan 2026) but its decoy-based anonymity (16-member rings) has a measured effective ring size of only ~4.2 against a statistical adversary, and the fix — FCMP++ (full-chain membership proofs, replacing rings with a Merkle/curve-tree membership proof over all outputs) — is still on a beta "stressnet" (v3.1 released 26 Oct 2026) with no mainnet fork date; a non-binding developer draft targets activation around March 2027. Real-world deanonymization has come mostly from network-layer IP harvesting (Chainalysis spy nodes), exchange KYC records, and user opsec, not from breaking the cryptography.

**Known deanonymization / weaknesses**
- Möser et al. (2017, revised 2018), "An Empirical Analysis of Traceability in the Monero Blockchain": ~62% of inputs with one or more mixins could be traced by chain-reaction elimination (pre-RingCT era), and the "guess-newest" heuristic identified the real input with ~80% accuracy. — [arXiv 1704.04299](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.04299)
- Rucknium's OSPEAD research estimates the average attack success probability against real users under the default decoy selection algorithm since the Aug 2022 fork is 23.5%, i.e. an effective ring size of ~4.2 (nominal 16; random guessing would be 6.25%); the proposed OSPEAD distribution would cut it to 7.6% (effective ring size ~13.2). OSPEAD "probably can only be safely implemented at a hard fork boundary." — [OSPEAD docs](https://rucknium.github.io/OSPEAD/CCS-milestone-2/OSPEAD-docs/_book); [OSPEAD performance evaluation](https://rucknium.github.io/OSPEAD/CCS-milestone-2/OSPEAD-docs/_book/performance-evaluation.html)
- March 2024 suspected spam ("black marble") attack: Rucknium's 28 Mar 2024 CCS proposal committed to analyze "the suspected transaction spam attack"; one of his simulations modeled a scenario where 59% of the decoy distribution mass was spammer outputs (a modeled scenario, not a measurement). — [CCS proposal](https://ccs.getmonero.org/proposals/Rucknium-Statistical-Research.html); [Rucknium gists](https://gist.github.com/Rucknium)
- EAE (Eve-Alice-Eve) / poisoned outputs: an attacker who transacts with the victim on both sides can recognize and exclude its own outputs when they appear as decoys; a 16-member ring with 12 attacker-controlled decoys has an effective anonymity set of ~4. Monero Academy calls this "feasible but expensive and conspicuous." — [Monero Academy, Decoy Selection, Flooding & Poisoned Outputs](https://moneroacademy.com/learn/attacks-and-defenses/decoy-and-poisoned-output-attacks)
- Leaked Chainalysis video (reported 9–10 Sept 2024): Cointelegraph obtained a deleted Chainalysis training video; it described running many Monero nodes across regions/ISPs to record transaction IPs and timestamps, with claims of tracing back to 2021; Chainalysis had "no comment." — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/chainalysis-leak-monero-traceability); [Crypto Times, 9 Sept 2024](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2024/09/09/leaked-video-suggests-monero-transactions-may-be-traceable/)
- Decrypt's fact-check (10 Sept 2024) describes it as a leaked Chainalysis presentation given to the IRS; Monero community member Csilla Brimer (MAGIC Monero Fund) said Chainalysis used "essentially fake proxy nodes" to harvest IPs and that "Without IP, they have nothing"; defense = run your own node / Tor. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/248728/monero-expert-fact-checks-chainalysis-video-claiming-xmr-transactions-can-be-traced)
- HN commenters on the video: the bigger claim was identifying the real output among 16 ring members, presumably via chain flooding; others judged the examples "very 'constructed' and unrealistic." — [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41554066)
- Finland's NBI (Vastaamo case, reported Jan 2024) claimed to trace XMR linked to Julius Kivimäki; the methods section of the report was withheld; critics argued the trail actually came via Binance records and opsec failures. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/finnish-authorities-traced-monero-vastaamo-hack); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/214367/did-law-enforcement-crack-privacy-coin-monero-its-complicated)

### Inferences
- The gap between nominal (16) and effective (~4.2) ring size is the single most important technical lesson: decoy-based anonymity is fragile to distribution-mismatch and flooding, and improvements require coordinated hard forks. FCMP++ (a Merkle-style membership proof over the whole output set) removes decoy selection as an attack surface, which is why Monero is migrating toward the Zcash-style model.
- Monero's exposure in practice is at the network layer (IP harvesting via spy nodes/proxies) and at fiat on/off-ramps, not the ring cryptography; any design should treat broadcast privacy (Dandelion++/Tor/I2P) and service-level data retention as first-class.
- Despite delistings since 2024, XMR's market cap roughly doubled from the Aug 2025 trough (~$248) to ~$600 (Nov 2026), suggesting delisting pressure has not killed demand but has pushed liquidity to DEX/non-custodial venues.

## Key Question 2: Zcash — Sapling/Orchard/Ironwood, Halo 2, shielded adoption surge, ZSAs, Tachyon, cross-chain, t-addr leakage, governance

### Takeaway
Zcash had the most eventful 2025–2026 of any privacy chain: a shielded-adoption surge (shielded supply ~29% of ZEC by Q3 2026, >50% of non-coinbase transactions touching shielded pools), a spot ETF (Grayscale ZCSH, ~$996M AUM by 25 Sept 2026), ZEC trading ~$1,200–1,260 in early Nov 2026 (~$20B market cap), but also a four-year-old counterfeiting bug in the Orchard circuit (found 29 May 2026) that forced a replacement "Ironwood" pool (NU6.3, 28 Jul 2026), the resignation of the entire Electric Coin Company team (7 Jan 2026), and NU7 (25-second blocks, Network Sustainability Mechanism) due on mainnet 5 Nov 2026. ZSAs are still not shipped; Tachyon (proof aggregation targeting ~50k private TPS) is in integration.

### Cited Findings

**Pools and the Orchard bug**
- Shielded Labs researcher Taylor Hornby discovered on 29 May 2026 a flaw in Orchard's proof circuit that could have let an attacker mint counterfeit ZEC without trace; it had been live since Orchard's launch in May 2022. Developers patched within days, restricted Orchard transactions via NU6.2 in June 2026, and retired Orchard with Ironwood (NU6.3) on 28 Jul 2026 at block 3,428,143; Orchard (≈3.66M ZEC, ≈$1.7B at ~$463) became exit-only with a turnstile capping withdrawals at verified deposits. — [Coinpaprika, 29 Jul 2026](https://coinpaprika.com/news/zcash-seals-17-billion-pool-four-year/)
- CoinDesk Research found evidence pointing away from exploitation (Orchard balance grew steadily, including during the 2025 rally), but "the fix cannot account for the past, because the zero-knowledge proofs hide what moved inside the pool." — [Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/zcash-seals-17-billion-pool-four-year/)
- Day-one Ironwood migration: ~176,000 ZEC (~$81M), ~5% of Orchard's balance; at that time transparent pool ≈12.5M ZEC, Sapling (2018) ≈582k, Orchard (2022) ≈3.5M. — [CoinDesk, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/29/about-usd80-million-zec-crosses-into-zcash-s-new-ironwood-pool-in-the-first-day)

## Key Question 4: Regulatory — EU AMLR Article 79, FATF, Japan/Korea/Dubai, delistings, optional vs mandatory privacy

### Takeaway
The binding deadline is 10 July 2027 (not 1 July), when Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 Article 79 bars EU CASPs from keeping anonymous accounts or accounts allowing anonymisation via "anonymity-enhancing coins"; the term is undefined, self-custody and non-custodial software are carved out, and the EBA/AMLA will decide how optionally-private assets (Zcash) are treated. Mandatory-privacy Monero has been delisted from Binance (2024), OKX (2024) and Kraken-EEA (2024) and is expected to be fully offboarded by EU CASPs, while optional-privacy Zcash kept Binance listing (Monitoring Tag removed July 2025), gained a US spot ETF (Aug 2026), and is expected to survive in the EU as transparent-only on exchanges.

### Cited Findings
- Article 79(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 (AMLR) bars credit institutions, financial institutions and CASPs from keeping anonymous accounts, including "crypto-asset accounts allowing anonymisation of transactions"; applies from 10 July 2027; "the 1 July 2027 date repeated across crypto media is wrong"; recitals 29–30 treat self-hosted wallet transactions as a risk factor, not prohibited. — [Soda Labs regulation hub](https://www.sodalabs.xyz/privacy-hub/regulation/reg-eu-amlr); [Leodex, updated 14 Jul 2026](https://leodex.io/learn/delistings/eu-privacy-coin-ban-2027)
- The prohibition "does not extend to providers of hardware and software, or to providers of self-hosted wallets, insofar as they do not possess access to or control over those wallets"; one source cites a later 10 July 2029 date for certain obliged entities under Article 3(3)(n)/(o) (unverified against the OJ text). — [Optimisus](https://optimisus.com/news/crypto-news/europe-bans-privacy-coin-custody-in-2027-and-the-exception-is-the-interesting-part/); [Didit](https://didit.me/blog/amlr-crypto-casp-requirements/)
- "Anonymity-enhancing coins" is not defined with precision; the EBA is drafting technical standards and AMLA (Frankfurt) will directly supervise up to 40 high-risk cross-border entities from ~July 2027; optionally-private assets are an open edge case. — [Leodex](https://leodex.io/learn/delistings/eu-privacy-coin-ban-2027); [Nomos Georgia](https://nomosgeorgia.com/eu-crypto-privacy-coin-ban-2027/)
- Expected EU outcome: Monero fully offboarded; Zcash may remain listed with transparent-only deposits/withdrawals and shielded support ending; Dash was delisted by several exchanges alongside XMR though Dash disputes being as private as Monero. — [Leodex](https://leodex.io/learn/delistings/eu-privacy-coin-ban-2027)
- The regulation "does not ban private ownership or self-hosted wallets." — [Blockonomi](https://blockonomi.com/monero-defies-delisting-wave-as-xmr-bull-flag-puts-799-peak-in-focus/)

### Inferences
- Regulators distinguish on custody and disclosure, not on cryptography: the EU rule bites on CASP-held accounts, FATF on travel-rule data, and both are satisfiable by a design that offers (a) transparent/KYC'd deposit-withdrawal surfaces and (b) user-controlled viewing/disclosure keys. Monero's lack of any such surface is why it is being structurally excluded, and why its ecosystem is shifting to DEX/P2P rails.
- Zcash's "optional privacy + viewing keys" produced materially better market outcomes (listings, ETF, ~2x Monero's market cap in Nov 2026) at the cost of a leaky t-addr boundary; the mitigation is to make shielded the default in wallets while keeping transparent as an interop edge (Zodl/Zashi's shielded-first design, Penumbra's UIP-8).
- The undefined term "anonymity-enhancing coins" plus pending EBA standards means designs that provide auditability (selective disclosure, viewing keys, proof-of-innocence style attestations) have a plausible path to EU compliance, but no guarantee before 2027.

## Key Question 5: Which ideas transfer to an Ethereum-based system

### Takeaway
The transferable, battle-tested ideas are: Merkle/note-commitment membership proofs over the full set (Zcash/Ironwood model, now Monero's FCMP++ destination) rather than decoy rings; viewing keys and selective-disclosure keys (Zcash, Firo Spark, Aleo) as the compliance interface; pool turnstiles and formal verification to contain circuit-soundness bugs (Ironwood); per-block batch swaps with public clearing price (Penumbra ZSwap) for MEV-resistant private trading; fuzzy message detection for light-client scanning without trusting a server (Penumbra); shielded rewards (Namada) as an incentive to grow the anonymity set — with the caveat that incentives did not produce durable TVL on Namada; and network-layer privacy (Dandelion++/Tor) because IP harvesting is how Monero users actually get caught.

### Cited Findings
- Full-set membership proofs vs decoys: Monero's FCMP++ replaces 16-member rings with proofs over all eligible outputs because effective ring size under the current decoy algorithm is ~4.2. — [OSPEAD docs](https://rucknium.github.io/OSPEAD/CCS-milestone-2/OSPEAD-docs/_book); [AD HOC NEWS](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/monero-price-steadies-as-fcmp-testing-keeps-upgrade-in-focus/70445705)
- Zcash anonymity sets for Sapling+Orchard were 124.5M note commitments at end of Q3 2026 (excluding Ironwood). — [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/34628)
- Viewing keys: read-only, cannot sign; cannot be revoked once shared; regulators may disagree on sufficiency. — [zkonomics](https://zkonomics.com/content/viewing-keys-explained/); [Grayscale](https://research.grayscale.com/reports/zcash-financial-privacy-in-the-age-of-ai)
- Aleo's view keys decrypt records for an auditor without spend authority; records now carry a sender_ciphertext decryptable by the recipient's view key. — [Equilibrium](https://equilibrium.co/writing/privacy-blockchains-and-aleo-deep-dive); [Provable](https://provable.com/blog/announcing-snarkOS-v4.0.0)
- Turnstiles bound damage: Orchard withdrawals capped at verifiably deposited amount after the counterfeiting bug; Ironwood shipped with a 2,700-theorem Lean proof and independent audits. — [Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/zcash-seals-17-billion-pool-four-year/); [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/34628)
- Privacy-preserving migration between pools via fixed denominations (ZIP-318, 0.1/0.5/1 ZEC). — [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/34628)
- Batch swaps: Penumbra executes all swaps in a block as one trade at a clearing price, removing per-trade ordering advantage; encrypted (sealed-bid) batches with validator threshold decryption of only the batch total are specified as a future upgrade (an encrypted-mempool analogue). — [Penumbra DEX spec](https://protocol.penumbra.zone/main/dex.html)
- Fuzzy message detection lets a light client outsource detection with sender-set false-positive rate and no false negatives; open issues on detector learning and adversarial N/M inflation. — [Penumbra FMD](https://protocol.penumbra.zone/main/crypto/fmd.html); [Considerations](https://protocol.penumbra.zone/main/crypto/fmd/considerations.html)
- Wallet-side PIR (Valar) for private sync currently caps near 1 TPS on Zcash — scanning/sync, not proving, is the scalability bottleneck; mobile proving is now ~200ms per shielded tx. — [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/34628)
- Proof aggregation per block (Tachyon) targets ~50k private TPS and ~100MB/s node data vs >500MB/s without recursion. — [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/34628)
- Shielded rewards: Namada pays NAM per epoch proportional to each governance-incentivized asset's shielded amount; design allots up to 10% of inflation; yet Namada TVL was ~$600K before the June 2026 exploit and ~$474 after. — [Namada docs](https://docs.namada.net/users/shielded-accounts/shielded-rewards); [Figment](https://www.figment.io/insights/namada-tokenomics-a-guide-to-nam-staking-rewards-and-privacy-features/); [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/protocol/namada-shielded-pools)

### Inferences
- For an Ethereum-based design: use note commitments + nullifiers with full-set Merkle membership (the model every serious project is converging on, including Monero), keep decoys only as an optional cheap-client fallback, and make the anonymity set shared across assets (MASP) so that small assets inherit the large set.
- Build compliance into keys, not into the protocol: incoming/outgoing viewing keys, per-transaction disclosure, and (ideally) revocable or time-bounded disclosure — a gap in both Zcash and Firo that regulators and users both dislike.
- Expect circuit bugs: segment pools with turnstiles, maintain supply-audit ability (Beam's "lustration," Zcash turnstile), and fund formal verification before TVL grows.
- Batch-per-block execution with a public clearing price gives private DEX functionality without an encrypted mempool; threshold-decrypted encrypted batches are the next step and remain unshipped even on Penumbra.
- Shielded rewards can bootstrap but do not retain liquidity; integrations with existing rails (stablecoins, exchange withdrawals, ETFs) correlate much better with adoption (Zcash, Aleo) than token incentives (Namada).
- Treat broadcast privacy and service-provider data retention (Wagyu leak, exchange KYC) as in-scope: most real deanonymization of Monero users came from there.
